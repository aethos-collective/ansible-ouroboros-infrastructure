# Ledger-Derived Playbooks

## Purpose

This document defines how Ansible playbooks may eventually be generated, selected, assembled, or parameterized from the Ouroboros Infrastructure operational ledger.

The current repository may contain handwritten playbooks. Those playbooks remain valuable as working lineage, bootstrap material, examples, reusable primitives, fallback paths, and operational memory.

## Core invariant

Playbooks are execution grammar.

They should eventually be derived from operational intent, execution contracts, substrate state, and reusable task or role corpora rather than treated as the sole permanent source of operational intent.

## Current mode

Current handwritten playbooks may express:

- bootstrap workflows;
- provisioning workflows;
- maintenance workflows;
- service deployment workflows;
- recovery workflows;
- inherited assumptions;
- prototype evidence;
- human-readable automation patterns.

These artifacts should be audited and classified before refactoring.

## Future mode

Future playbooks may be:

- generated from intent;
- selected from known-good playbook libraries;
- assembled from smaller task fragments;
- parameterized from ledger state;
- constrained by execution contracts;
- specialized to a holon, node class, service class, or recovery path;
- materialized as check-only views or apply-capable views.

## Derivation inputs

Playbook derivation may use:

- operational intent records;
- execution contracts;
- node capability records;
- service binding records;
- topology records;
- inventory derivation results;
- role and task corpora;
- policy and review posture;
- secrets eligibility;
- expected rollback or recovery posture.

## Derivation modes

### Selection

Choose an existing playbook that already matches the intent and scope.

### Assembly

Compose a playbook from reusable roles, tasks, includes, or templates.

### Parameterization

Bind ledger-derived variables to a known playbook shape.

### Generation

Generate a new playbook artifact from a higher-level operational intent.

Generation must remain inspectable and reviewable.

## Review posture

Derived playbooks must preserve review before apply wherever possible.

A derived playbook should support:

- inspection;
- diff review;
- check mode;
- provenance review;
- operator halt;
- expiration or staleness detection;
- archival after execution.

## Provenance requirements

A derived playbook artifact should answer:

- what operational intent produced it;
- what execution contract it serves;
- what inventory view it targets;
- what role/task corpus it used;
- which component generated, selected, assembled, or parameterized it;
- which holon requested it;
- which human or ÆI participated;
- what review posture applies;
- whether apply posture is authorized;
- what result returned after execution.

## Safety rule

The mesh must not produce opaque playbooks that operators cannot understand.

A playbook generated by the system must be at least as inspectable as a handwritten playbook.

## Relationship to StorageGraft

Derived playbooks may be materialized by StorageGraft into an execution-specific filesystem view.

They may appear inside an Ansible Runner-compatible `project/` directory or another adapter-specific execution layout.

## Non-goals

This document does not implement playbook generation.

It does not modify existing playbooks.

It does not authorize apply posture for generated playbooks.

It defines the future derivation model for playbooks.
